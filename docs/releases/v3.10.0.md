# v3.10.0 — Approximate word highlighting that follows the spoken pace

**Source update on `main`.** The packaged GitHub release remains v3.9.0. Use the [current source ZIP](https://github.com/aolingge/yt-dual-subs/archive/refs/heads/main.zip) or a checkout. [Install guide](../../README.md#install) · [中文](#中文)

- Approximate word highlighting now distributes words by **syllables** instead of character count. A long compound such as "Streichholzschächtelchen" counts 5 syllables while "und" counts 1, so the highlight no longer lingers on long words.
- Spoken numbers get the time they actually take: "1990" counts 6 syllables ("neunzehnhundertneunzig"), and a group like "19:30" holds several times the time of a short word.
- Punctuation opens a real pause between words — sentence end, then dash, colon and comma — and abbreviations such as "z. B." no longer read as sentence ends.
- The estimator first measures **this video's own pace** from any cues that already carry real word times, and uses that for the rest of the video. A per-language default (German 215 ms per syllable) applies only when fewer than three cues are timed.
- A cue holding trailing silence no longer stretches the words across it: words never spread past 1.4× their natural length, and a cue too short for its text is squeezed to fit.
- No install, no helper process, no new permission and no new dependency. This stays local, display-only estimation, labeled **Approximate** in the video; raw captions and SRT exports are unchanged.

All **160 source regression tests** in 15 files passed, including 5 new cases for syllable counts, number handling, the "z. B." abbreviation guard, the trailing-silence cap and the measured video pace. In isolated Edge, with a German track that has sentence times only and no same-language automatic track, the highlight moved through all 11 words of "Wir treffen uns um 19:30 Uhr, danach gehen wir essen.": the comma gave a 0.45s gap versus 0.30s after a bare short word, and "19:30" held 2.0s against 0.30s for "um". In a 6-second cue holding only about 3 seconds of speech, the second word started 0.65s in, where a cue-filling estimate would place it around 1.3s.

It is still an estimate, and no audio is analyzed: a speaker who pauses mid-sentence, or reads a number differently from the syllable count, will not match exactly. The video's own pace can only be measured when its captions include word times; otherwise the per-language default is used.

Reload the extension in its existing unpacked folder and refresh open YouTube tabs to activate the updated scripts.

## 中文

**这是 `main` 分支的源码更新。** GitHub 发行包仍为 v3.9.0；请使用[最新源码 ZIP](https://github.com/aolingge/yt-dual-subs/archive/refs/heads/main.zip)或 Git 克隆。[安装说明](../../README.zh-CN.md#安装)

- 近似跟读改为按**音节**分配时间，不再按字母数。像 „Streichholzschächtelchen“ 这样的长词算 5 个音节，而 „und“ 只算 1 个，高亮不会长时间停在长词上。
- 读数字会得到它真正需要的时间：„1990“ 算 6 个音节（neunzehnhundertneunzig），„19:30“ 这类数字组占用的时间可达短词的好几倍。
- 标点会真正产生词间停顿——句末、破折号、冒号、逗号依次递减；„z. B.“ 这类缩写不再被当成句末。
- 估算会先**从本视频已有词时间的字幕里量出这个人的语速**，再用于同一视频的其他句子；只有带词时间的句子少于三句时才使用按语言设定的默认值（德语每音节 215 毫秒）。
- 句子结尾包含停顿的 cue 不再把词硬拉满整句：词的时间最多拉伸到自然长度的 1.4 倍，句子过短的 cue 则整体压缩以适应。
- 无需安装任何东西、没有本机程序、不新增权限和依赖。这仍是纯本地的显示层估算，画面标注**近似跟读**；原始字幕与 SRT 导出不受影响。

全部 **15 个文件的 160 项源码回归测试**通过，其中 5 项新测试覆盖音节计数、数字处理、„z. B.“ 缩写判断、句尾停顿上限与本视频实测语速。在独立 Edge 中，使用只有句子时间、且没有同语言自动字幕的德语音轨，高亮依次走过 „Wir treffen uns um 19:30 Uhr, danach gehen wir essen.“ 的全部 11 个词：逗号处停顿 0.45 秒，而普通短词后只有 0.30 秒；„19:30“ 占用 2.0 秒，„um“ 只有 0.30 秒。在一个 6 秒却只说了约 3 秒的 cue 中，第二个词在 0.65 秒就开始，而按整句平摊的估算会把它放在约 1.3 秒。

这仍然是估算，且不分析音频：说话人中途停顿，或把数字读成另一种形式时，不会完全对上。只有字幕本身带词时间时才能量出该视频的语速，否则使用按语言的默认值。

在原来的解压目录重新加载扩展，再刷新已打开的 YouTube 标签页，使新脚本生效。
