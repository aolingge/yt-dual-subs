# v3.11.0 — Word times from three sources: captions, matched automatic captions, and optional audio alignment

**Source update on `main`.** The packaged GitHub release remains v3.9.0. Use the [current source ZIP](https://github.com/aolingge/yt-dual-subs/archive/refs/heads/main.zip) or a checkout. [Install guide](../../README.md#install) · [中文](#中文)

- A sentence no longer loses all of its word times because one word differs. When the whole line cannot be matched against the same-language automatic captions, reliable **contiguous fragments** (at least two consecutive words, on one diagonal, with times inside the cue) are used as anchors and only the words between them are estimated. Such a line is labelled **Partly matched** and its estimated words carry a dashed underline, so a mixed line is never presented as an exact match.
- Matching now compares numbers and abbreviated spellings without touching what is displayed: `1.000`, `1,000` and `1000` are the same number, `1,5` equals `1.5`, and `z. B.` equals `z.b.`. Repeated phrases matched in more than one place, ambiguous full matches, dropped or rewritten words, and anchor times outside the caption window are refused instead of being force-fitted.
- The estimated pace now follows the speaker instead of using one value for the whole video: the nearest reliable word starts (12 seconds around the sentence, widening once to 45 seconds) decide the local pace, with the video-wide median and then the per-language default as fallbacks. A median with outlier rejection keeps a single long pause or unusual cue from skewing it, and the measurement is computed once per caption track instead of on every frame.
- **Optional audio alignment** is available for German and English. A local helper aligns the original captions to the video's audio with a Wav2Vec2 model and returns a start **and** an end for every word. It runs only on `127.0.0.1`, uses no account cookies, downloads no paid service, and is started only by you. Captions, highlighting and translation keep working when it is not running.
- Every result is bound to what it was computed from: video id, source language, a hash of the caption track (text, boundaries and order), the model and helper version, and the audio start offset. If any of those change, the old result is refused and the popup reports it as stale instead of applying word times to a different video or track. Switching video or subtitle track cancels the running job.
- A local audio file can be imported when the video's audio cannot be fetched. Its **start time** in the video must be given, and a mismatch is refused loudly (`audioTooShort`, `audioMismatch`) instead of being silently applied; audio never leaves the computer.
- Settings gained an explicit **Word-time source** choice — **Automatic** (prefer reliable times, estimate the rest), **Approximate follow-along** (no extra track fetch and no model), and **Audio alignment**. The status line distinguishes caption times, automatic match, partial match, approximate follow-along, audio analysis running, audio analysis failed with a recovery step, and stale results. No source is described as 100% accurate. Preference saves still go through the existing staged worker queue, so no `MAX_WRITE_OPERATIONS_PER_MINUTE` regression.
- Silence is respected: when audio timing is available and a gap between two words is reliably longer than the pause threshold, the previous word stops being highlighted instead of staying lit until the next word.

The **189 source tests in 17 files** and the helper's **11 Python tests** pass. The new cases cover partial anchoring with per-word provenance, repetition and ambiguity rejection, number/abbreviation normalization, leading and trailing fill inside a caption, a two-speed track, a long silence inside one cue, window widening and give-up behaviour, the one-measurement-per-track cache, mode switching without a reload, refused stale audio records, a failed analysis that leaves captions and translation usable, a caption-track identity change, and adjacent captions that share no audio.

Real speech was verified with the real helper and the real German model on 12 public-domain German recordings (Thorsten corpus, 78 words): every word received a start and an end, no time left its caption window, word order stayed strictly increasing, per-word confidence was 0.89–1.00, and 8 of 9 shuffled transcripts were rejected rather than given believable times. Alignment cost 97–453 ms per caption on CPU, and the model loaded in 13.4 s once cached. The aligned span sat inside each recording — the first word started 80–303 ms after the audio began and the last ended about 304 ms before it — with the strongest compression on captions shorter than about 2 s, so these boundaries are an aid rather than measured speech onset.

**Not verified:** absolute per-word error against human-checked word boundaries, more than one speaker, and the live anonymous YouTube audio download, which this network could not complete (the local proxy truncated the media stream and YouTube later demanded sign-in). Treat audio alignment as a strong aid for German and English captions, not as a guarantee.

Reload the extension in its existing unpacked folder and refresh open YouTube tabs to activate the updated scripts. Audio alignment additionally needs the local helper: see [AUDIO_ALIGNMENT.md](../AUDIO_ALIGNMENT.md).

## 中文

**这是 `main` 分支的源码更新。** GitHub 发行包仍为 v3.9.0；请使用[最新源码 ZIP](https://github.com/aolingge/yt-dual-subs/archive/refs/heads/main.zip)或 Git 克隆。[安装说明](../../README.zh-CN.md#安装)

- 整句里只要有一个词不同，不再让整句退回估算。当整句无法与同语言自动字幕匹配时，程序会找出可靠的**连续词片段**（至少连续两个词、位于同一对角线、时间落在本句范围内）作为锚点，只估算锚点之间的词。这类句子会标注为**部分匹配 + 估算**，估算的词带虚线下划线，混合来源的句子不会被当成完全匹配。
- 匹配时的规范化不再影响显示内容：`1.000`、`1,000` 与 `1000` 视为同一个数，`1,5` 等于 `1.5`，`z. B.` 等于 `z.b.`。重复短语匹配到多处、整句匹配有歧义、漏词或改写、锚点时间越出本句范围的情况都会拒绝套用，而不是强行对时。
- 语速估算改为跟随说话人，不再整条视频用一个值：优先使用句子周围 12 秒内的可靠词起点（必要时扩大到 45 秒），再退回全片中位数，最后才用按语言的默认值。中位数并剔除异常值，个别长停顿或异常句子不会带偏结果；每条字幕轨只测量一次，不再每帧重扫。
- 新增**可选音频对齐**，支持德语和英语。本机辅助程序用 Wav2Vec2 模型把原文字幕与视频音频对齐，为每个词给出开始**和**结束时间。程序只监听 `127.0.0.1`，不使用账号 Cookie，不接入付费服务，且只由你手动启动；未启动时字幕、跟读和翻译照常工作。
- 每个结果都与它的来源绑定：视频标识、语言、字幕轨哈希（文字、边界与顺序）、模型与辅助程序版本、音频起始偏移。任一项变化后，旧结果会被拒绝并在弹窗中标记为已过期，不会把词时间套到别的视频或字幕上。切换视频或字幕轨会取消正在运行的任务。
- 无法获取视频音频时，可以导入对应的本地音频文件。必须填写它在视频中的**起始时间**；不一致时会明确拒绝（`audioTooShort`、`audioMismatch`），不会静默套用；音频不会离开本机。
- 设置中新增明确的**词时间来源**选择——**自动**（优先可靠时间，其余估算）、**近似跟读**（不额外抓取字幕轨、不使用模型）、**音频对齐**。状态行会区分字幕词时间、自动字幕匹配、部分匹配、近似跟读、音频对齐处理中、音频对齐失败（并提示恢复方式）以及结果过期。任何来源都不会被描述为 100% 准确。设置保存仍走原有的排队机制，不会再次出现 `MAX_WRITE_OPERATIONS_PER_MINUTE`。
- 静音会被尊重：有音频时间且词与词之间的间隔明显超过停顿阈值时，上一个词会停止高亮，不会一直亮到下一个词。

**17 个文件的 189 项源码测试**与辅助程序的 **11 项 Python 测试**全部通过。新增测试覆盖：带逐词来源的部分锚定、重复与歧义拒绝、数字与缩写规范化、句首句尾补全、快慢语速变化、单句内长静音、窗口扩大与放弃逻辑、每条字幕轨只测量一次、切换模式无需刷新、过期音频结果被拒、分析失败后字幕与翻译仍可用、字幕轨身份变化，以及相邻字幕不共用音频。

真实语音验证使用真实辅助程序与真实德语模型，覆盖 12 段公有领域德语录音（Thorsten 语料，78 个词）：每个词都得到开始与结束时间，时间都落在本句窗口内，词序严格递增，逐词置信度 0.89–1.00，打乱词序后有 9 句中的 8 句被拒绝，而不是给出看似合理的时间。CPU 上每句耗时 97–453 毫秒，模型缓存后加载 13.4 秒。对齐范围落在录音内部——首词在音频开始后 80–303 毫秒处开始，末词约在结束前 304 毫秒结束——短于约 2 秒的句子压缩最明显，因此这些边界是辅助参考，不等于实测的发音起点。

**未验证：** 与人工逐词边界对比的绝对误差、多人讲话，以及匿名 YouTube 音频的在线下载（本机网络无法完成：本地代理会截断媒体流，之后 YouTube 要求登录）。请把音频对齐视为德语和英语字幕的有力辅助，而不是保证。

在原来的解压目录重新加载扩展，再刷新已打开的 YouTube 标签页，使新脚本生效。音频对齐还需要本机辅助程序：见 [AUDIO_ALIGNMENT.md](../AUDIO_ALIGNMENT.md)。
